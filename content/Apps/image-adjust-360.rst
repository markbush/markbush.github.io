Image Adjust 360 is an application which allows you to adjust the default front view of 360º equirectangular images from your Photos library.

If you've ever tried to view your 360º images to discover the main subject is to the side or behind you, then this application is for you!  Use it to rotate your images so that the main subject is correctly positioned at the front.

By default, the application shows all of your 360º images in your Photos library, however you can also browse for them by album.

When you select an image, you can rotate it left and right until the main subject is in the centre.  You can either save the result as a new image or overwrite the original.

Note: when overwriting an image, Image Adjust 360 does so non-destructively!  This means you can always revert the change in the Photos app at any time.

Use the "Cancel" button to abandon edits to the current image and "Save" to save any changes made.  The left and right arrows allow you to move between images in the collection.  If there are unsaved changes to the current image, you'll be asked if you want to save them.  Just tap away from the popup if you want to cancel the action.

This application works on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro.

Privacy Policy
--------------

The application Image Adjust 360 does not collect, store, or share any data.

Image Adjust 360 accesses and processes your photos from the Photos app in order to display them as thumbnails in a window and to allow you to edit them.  Photos are not stored in the app, but are accessed directly from and saved back to the Photos app every time.

Your photos remain secure.
